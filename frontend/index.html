About the project

Why this assistant was created

Pakistan's laws and constitutional rights are important, but legal language can be difficult to search and understand. Citizens often need a simple starting point before they know which law, institution or professional to approach.

This project was created to reduce that information gap. It combines artificial intelligence with retrieval from official legal documents so that complex material can be explained in professional, accessible language without requiring the user to know formal legal terminology.

Purpose and impact

What problem does it solve?

The assistant is designed as an educational first step - not a replacement for a lawyer.

01

Plain-language understanding

Turns difficult legal and constitutional wording into clear explanations for ordinary users.

02

Natural conversation

Understands questions written naturally in English, Urdu and Roman Urdu.

03

Faster legal orientation

Helps users identify relevant rights, possible next steps and questions to discuss with a qualified lawyer.

04

Responsible AI guidance

Separates source-grounded answers from general AI knowledge and avoids presenting unverified claims as confirmed law.
